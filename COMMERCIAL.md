`recall/` is MIT-licensed and free for any use, commercial or not — only **Recall Pro** requires a commercial licence.

---

## What counts as commercial use?

You need a commercial licence if **any** of the following apply:

- You use Recall Pro inside a for-profit company (commercial product, internal tools, agency work)
- You use Recall Pro as part of a paid service offering
- Recall Pro contributes to revenue you earn, directly or indirectly
- You bundle Recall Pro into a commercial product
- You use Recall Pro for client work where you bill by the hour or project

## What does **not** require a commercial licence?

- Personal use on your own machine for your own projects
- Evaluation (try it before deciding to buy)
- Academic / research use
- Contributing to the Recall Pro project itself
- Use inside a registered non-profit or charity
- Use by an individual developer learning or experimenting

When in doubt, ask.
